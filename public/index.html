About

I graduated from the Computer Programmer Analyst (Co-op) program at Niagara College in January 2017. Over those three years, I got the chance to work on all kinds of projects—web, mobile, and Windows-based applications—while also building up my communication skills and learning a lot about computer hardware.

These days, I’m a Developer at iPipeline Canada, where I’m part of a diverse team creating advanced web applications for the insurance and investment industry. In the past, I’ve worked at Niagara Research in the Agriculture & Environmental Technologies Innovation Centre, where I developed a single-page web app to help farmers track and analyze crop growth.

When I’m not working, you can usually find me at the gym, practicing guitar or spending quality time with close friends and family.
